# Assembly

## 1. Set the magnets (do this first)

Every magnet goes in with its **north face pointing up**, towards the top of the robot. That way each pair attracts.

The easy way to get it right every time:

1. Glue a magnet into a pocket on the first part.
2. Let a second magnet snap onto it.
3. Put a drop of glue in the matching pocket of the second part.
4. Press the second part onto the stacked magnet and hold for 30 seconds.

The second magnet ends up facing the correct way automatically. Mark the north faces with a marker before you start.

| Joint | Pockets |
|---|---|
| Lid ↔ base | 4 in the lid top, 4 in the base corner posts |
| Base ↔ rack | 4 in the base deck, 4 under the rack floor |
| Rack ↔ shell | 4 on the rack crown, 4 in the shell ceiling pads |

## 2. Collar pins

Push four 5 mm pieces of 1.75 mm filament into the pin holes on top of the base. The collar drops onto them.

## 3. Load the rack

1. **OLED:** screw it to the front carrier with 4 × M2 screws, with the header pins at the top. Feed the wires through the slot above it.
2. **LED:** push it into the hole under the screen from behind.
3. **ESP32-C3 + expansion board:** on the shelf, USB-C facing the back.
4. **Speaker:** slide it between the two ribs on the right frame, cone facing out.
5. **Amp:** on the inside of the rear wall, above the USB port.
6. **SW420:** on the left frame.
7. **Mic and touch:** on top of the crown. The mic goes at the front, under the pinhole. Route both cables down through the crown slot.
8. **Switch:** in the bracket at the back, next to the USB-C port.

Wire everything as shown in [Wiring](wiring.md). Double-sided foam tape or a dab of hot glue holds the small modules well.

## 4. Stack it

1. Snap the lid onto the base (with or without the battery).
2. Plug the battery lead through the base and rack slots into the expansion board.
3. Put the collar on its pins.
4. Lower the rack into the collar; it snaps to the base.
5. Lower the shell over the rack; it snaps to the crown and clamps the collar.

To open it, pull the shell straight up.
